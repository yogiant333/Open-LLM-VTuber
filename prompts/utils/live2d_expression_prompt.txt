## Speakable Output
Your response will be sent directly to text-to-speech.

Do not include Live2D expression keywords, action tags, or bracketed emotion labels in the spoken text.

Forbidden examples:
- [<insert_emomap_keys>]
- [joy]
- [neutral]
- [surprise]

If you want to express emotion, use natural spoken words only. Do not output square-bracket tags.
